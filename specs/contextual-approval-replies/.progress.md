## Learnings

- The helper and schema copies are byte-identical and Bats asserts that parity; update them together rather than creating surface-specific resolvers.
- Pre-delegation already has one authoritative pending confirmation ID, but artifact approval has only `awaitingApproval`; a persisted one-action descriptor is needed for safe contextual resolution and resume.
- Keep `classify-reply` unchanged for active interview questions. Resolve contextual approval only at an explicitly persisted single-action gate, then retain the existing confirm, manifest, delegation, and writer checks.
- No new dependency is needed. Focused Bats coverage should exercise affirmative acceptance, question/quoted/revision rejection, multi-action no-op, audit fields, and resume.
- Requirements scope is limited to one state-aware resolver/audit path: pre-delegation uses its pending confirmation ID; artifact advancement and revision dispatch require one persisted descriptor, with revision feedback already recorded.
- Observable Bats coverage must prove accepted affirmatives, every fail-closed rejection class, audit fields, resume behavior, helper/schema parity, and applicable release metadata updates.
- Design selects one `resolve-approval` helper command, an optional one-object `approvalGate`, and helper-owned append-only `approvalAudit`; no NLP or alternate delegation path is needed.
- A descriptor must match the current phase and `awaitingApproval`; revision descriptors carry recorded feedback, while multi-option artifact prompts intentionally persist no descriptor and require clarification.
